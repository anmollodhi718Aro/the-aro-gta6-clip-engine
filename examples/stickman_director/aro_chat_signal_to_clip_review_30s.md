# THE ARO | From Chat Signal to Clip Review

**POC status:** storyboard draft only; not yet owner-approved for asset generation or rendering  
**Target:** 30 seconds, 16:9 landscape  
**Visual direction:** B1 Minimal Line Art, adapted as original black-and-white stickman artwork  
**Mode:** manual storyboard/treatment only; no paid generation, external assets, gameplay footage, or upload

## Creative brief

Explain how THE ARO turns a burst of livestream chat activity into a reviewable clip candidate while keeping local-media rights and human approval in control. The video is a product explainer, not a gameplay montage.

## Original voiceover script

> A busy chat doesn't automatically mean a great moment. THE ARO spots unusual activity and turns it into a timestamped candidate. We match that moment to an authorized local recording, then check rights and clip quality. Finally, a human reviews the cut. Nothing gets published without explicit approval.

Approximate delivery: 30 seconds at a calm, clear pace. Keep the voiceover original; no imitation of a real creator or public figure.

## Visual and asset constraints

- Draw all stickman characters, chat bubbles, graphs, icons, and UI panels as original assets.
- Use abstract interface shapes only. Do not reproduce YouTube UI, GTA 6 logos, game characters, maps, or copyrighted gameplay.
- Do not use scraped livestreams, third-party creator footage, music, sound effects, voices, fonts, or stock assets.
- If any third-party asset is proposed later, record its source, license, and permitted use before it enters the project.
- Keep the rights-check and human-review gates visible. Never depict automatic upload or public publishing.
