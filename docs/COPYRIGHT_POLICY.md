# Copyright, Creator Permission, and Source Policy

## Required before processing

Record, at minimum:
- creator/source identity
- person or organization granting permission
- permission evidence reference (do not store private credentials)
- permitted uses (analysis, clipping, editing, distribution)
- allowed platforms and territories, if relevant
- start date, expiry/review date, and revocation status
- any attribution, monetization, or content restrictions

Do not process a source marked unknown, expired, revoked, or out of scope.

## Permission is not a universal rights clearance

A creator's permission may not cover:
- music or sound recordings owned by third parties
- game publisher or other embedded intellectual-property restrictions
- guest appearances, private information, or other third-party material
- platform terms or API restrictions

Check each applicable license, permission, and platform rule before export or distribution. When rights are uncertain, do not publish.

## Source acquisition rules

- Use official APIs or interfaces that explicitly permit the intended access.
- Do not bypass access controls, DRM, paywalls, privacy settings, or platform restrictions.
- Do not scrape or rip livestream video.
- Use an owned or separately authorized media file for video processing.
- API access to metadata or chat must never be treated as permission to obtain the video.

## Review and takedown

- Keep publishing disabled by default.
- Require a human to review the clip, title, captions, attribution, and rights status.
- Provide a way to pause processing and revoke a source's permission.
- On a credible rights complaint, pause related processing and distribution, preserve only the minimum audit details needed, and follow the applicable takedown process.

This document is an engineering policy, not legal advice. Obtain appropriate legal guidance before public commercial distribution.
